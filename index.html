    How to play Daily Red

    1. Get your team. A new party of six is available for every date. The expanded pool has 214 species.
    2. Pick your lead and battle. Choose a move each turn, or switch Pokémon when the matchup calls for it.
    3. Try another challenge. Choose an opponent and variant, or start a fresh practice run. Your battles are saved in this browser.

    FAQ

    Can I still battle the original Daily Red?

    Yes. Red → Daily Red uses the uploaded game's original opponent. The expanded pool is selected by default; the original player pool is also available.

    Does the same date give the same team?

    Yes, for the same date, opponent and party settings. Opponent-balanced parties are checked against the selected opponent. Unrestricted random uses the same dated draw across opponents. Practice uses a fresh recorded seed.

    Does this affect my daily-red.com record?

    No. These battles run locally and are saved separately. About this version.
